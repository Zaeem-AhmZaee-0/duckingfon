# DuckingFon case study preparation

This is a structure for a later evidence-backed case study, not a claim of user research or finished performance.

1. Problem: making forecast information understandable through a companion and an environment.
2. Concept: Fon experiences selected-location weather and time.
3. Visual direction: supplied logo, blue/white/yellow character, restrained atmosphere.
4. Fon: distinguish supplied reference, temporary proxy, and later final rig.
5. Weather State Engine: provider normalization and honest unavailable values.
6. Life Engine: weighted behavior, cooldowns and severity interrupts.
7. World State Director: target states and continuous interpolation.
8. Interaction design: search, forecasts, globe, radar and saved places.
9. Design system: semantic controls, typography, contrast, settings and motion.
10. Development: actual architecture, API tradeoffs, tests and resolved defects.
11. Responsive experience: screenshots from verified viewport sizes.
12. Accessibility: tested keyboard/focus/reduced-motion paths; do not claim certification.
13. Performance: measured bundle/asset sizes and renderer metrics; no invented FPS or conversion gains.
14. Final experience: capture after graphics-enabled visual QA and final model integration.

Evidence belongs in QA.md and test outputs. Do not invent interviews, engagement metrics, business results, or claims that the proxy is the final Fon.
